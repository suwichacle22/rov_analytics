# rov_analytics

## Dashboard (apps/web)

- No subtitles. A card title or a section heading stands alone: do not put a line of explanation under it. `Card` and `Section` in `apps/web/src/components/ui.tsx` take a title only.
- What a reader needs in order to read a chart goes into the chart itself: column headers, a short legend line at the bottom of the card, or the tooltip of a row.
