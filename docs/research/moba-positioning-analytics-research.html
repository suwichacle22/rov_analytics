How MOBA analytics measure movement and positioning, and how to adapt it to Arena of Valor

Goal: a coaching tool that shows how competitive RoV players walk (pathing) and where and how long they stay (positioning). Sources cover Dota 2, League of Legends, and Honor of Kings research, commercial tools, and minimap computer vision.

1. Key conclusions

No position data source exists for Arena of Valor. Tencent, TiMi, Garena and Level Infinite publish no match API. In-game .abc replays are encrypted, version-locked, and nobody has parsed them. An API-based design like the League of Legends tools is not possible.

The realistic data path is minimap computer vision

Read hero icons off the minimap from broadcast VODs (RPL, AWC, AIC, KPL) or screen-recorded replays. DeepLeague, PandaScore and pyLoL did this for LoL, and the 2026 Dota2-Vis paper did it for Dota. The recipe transfers directly.

The analytics layer is well understood

Dota 2 and Honor of Kings research defines a small set of metrics (zone dwell, zone changes, within-team distance, convex hull, proximity, encounters) that separate pros from amateurs. They only need rescaling to AoV's smaller map and 12 to 18 minute games.

Nobody ships this for mobile MOBAs

Tencent's 王者营地 (Honor of Kings Camp) app has an animated movement-route replay, but only for your own matches, on the Chinese server, inside the app. That gap is the opportunity.

Sampling at 1 to 2 Hz is enough

Every coaching metric works at one position per second. Two per second helps tracking through teamfights. Above 5 Hz adds nothing.

2. Data sources for AoV and HoK, ranked

1
Broadcast and VOD minimap computer vision
RPL on Garena RoV Thailand YouTube and garena.live, AWC and AIC on AoV Esports YouTube, KPL on Huya, Douyu and Bilibili. Public, 1080p, every pro match. Observer view usually has full vision. Overlays sometimes cover the minimap.
2
Screen-recorded in-game replay
AoV added replays around v1.44 (Dec 2021). Cleanest frames, but player-perspective replays have fog of war. Verify whether the current Garena client offers a global or observer camera in replay.
3
Abios Hermes "Honor of Kings Computer Vision Channels"
Commercial CV feed derived from broadcasts. Published channels are events, gold and kills. Positions would have to be requested. Enterprise pricing.
4
王者营地 private API scrape
Tencent's backend stores per-hero movement routes (animated review since Jan 2019). Unofficial, session-bound, Chinese HoK only, violates terms. Not applicable to Garena RoV.
5
hok_env and Hokoff (Tencent AI Lab)
1v1 and 3v3 reinforcement-learning environments with position features in the observation vector. Gated license. Useful only as a schema reference.
6
.abc replay files
1 to 2 MB, encrypted, version-locked, almost certainly a lockstep input log that needs the engine to re-simulate. No parser exists.
7
Liquipedia, KPL data portal, Esports Charts
Draft, results, KDA, viewership. No positions. Use to join match context onto tracks.

Anti-cheat note

Recording your own screen (built-in recorder, scrcpy or ADB mirroring, MediaProjection, ReplayKit) is not flagged by Tencent ACE. Do not run the game in a cloned or virtualised container, and do not inject overlays. Pro matches come from public VODs, so this is moot there.

AoV recalibration

Scale distance thresholds as a fraction of map width rather than copying LoL units. Use a window like 1:30 to 8:00 instead of 3:00 to 15:00, since AoV games last 12 to 18 minutes.

5. Visualisations coaches actually use

  • 2D replay with timeline scrubber and event jump (Shadow.gg, STRATZ playback, Prolytics). The most-used artefact by pro analysts.
  • Minimap heatmap per player, role, or game phase, as KDE or 2D histogram (StratIncon, OpenDota, STRATZ ward maps).
  • Trajectory overlay with a time-coloured polyline.
  • Zone grid choropleth of dwell time, using 11 hand zones per Drachen or the 12x12 grid per Tencent.
  • Numbered camp or waypoint sequence on the minimap with sample counts (OP.GG and LeagueOfGraphs jungle paths).
  • "Where were comparable pros at minute N" overlay and next-position win-rate heatmap (Doran's Lab).
  • Within-team distance and hull-area line charts aligned to objective spawns.
  • Two-player path comparison view.
  • Event glyphs on the timeline for encounters, recalls, and inconsistencies.

7. Minimap computer vision: what works

ProjectMethodLesson
DeepLeague (2018)YOLOv2 on 100k auto-labeled 1080p minimap cropsPoor generalisation to unseen champions, heavy labeling. Do not copy the data strategy.
PandaScore (2019)Per-champion classifier plus ConvLSTM for temporal memory. GAN-generated minimap artefacts on raw portraits.Realistic artefacts (rings, pings, overlaps) matter more than naive augmentation. ConvLSTM solves occlusion. Over 95% within 20 px.
Maknee part 1Pure OpenCV: HSV ring colour, contours, circle fit, template matchZero-training baseline. Fragile to pings and overlaps.
Maknee part 2Faster R-CNN trained on purely synthetic minimaps rendered from icon assets. 150 hand-labeled frames for eval only.AP@0.5 of 0.999, about 80% within 5 px, 20 to 30 fps. Zero real training labels.
pyLoLYOLOv12 on portraits from Riot CDN pasted onto minimap backgrounds. Drives the replay client at 40x, samples 1 per s, outputs JSON.Closest architectural template for this tool. 92.2% mAP, no manual labels.
boboyes dataset and YOLOv11Synthetic generator: fog masks, recall and teleport effects, ping waves, viewport rectangle, JPEG noise, icon clusteringGood augmentation checklist.
Dota2-Vis (2026)YOLO11 on 2,477 hand-annotated 240x240 minimap crops from TI2025 VODs, both team perspectivesAbout 2.5k real labels gives F1 0.939. Names Honor of Kings as a target.

No published minimap tracker exists for AoV or HoK. This would be the first public one.

Technique ranking, simplest that works first

1

Colour ring plus template matching, zero training. HSV threshold on the team ring colour, contours, circle radius gate, then template match against the 10 drafted heroes only, known from the loading screen. Gets most frames.

2

Small YOLO on synthetic minimap crops. Render AoV icon assets onto the minimap background with team rings, overlaps, pings, recall circles, death skulls, fog masks, JPEG noise and scale jitter. 20k to 50k images. Restrict inference classes to the 10 drafted heroes.

3

Temporal tracking. Per-hero constant-velocity Kalman filter with Hungarian assignment. Identity comes from the classifier, so no re-identification is needed. Hold the last position for a few seconds on a missed detection and flag low confidence.

4

Occlusion heuristic. If hero A vanishes while hero B is within one icon radius, assign A to B's position with an occluded flag.

5

Pixel to map coordinates. The minimap is axis-aligned, so an affine map (crop origin plus scale) suffices. Normalise to a unit square. Calibrate per video by template-matching fixed landmarks such as cores and towers. This also auto-detects the minimap crop box.

6

Game clock OCR. Crop the timer, threshold, read with Tesseract or PaddleOCR digit mode, or trust the offset from a detected 00:00.

Pipeline sketch

VOD (mp4 / YouTube)
  - ffmpeg -vf "fps=2" frames/%06d.png
    - locate minimap once per video (landmark template match) -> crop box
      - per frame: crop -> 256x256 -> detector (YOLO) or OpenCV baseline
        - per-hero Kalman + occlusion heuristic -> (t, hero, x, y, conf, occluded)
          - Parquet/CSV: match_id, t_sec, team, hero, player, x_norm, y_norm, conf
            - analytics: 2D histogram / KDE heatmaps per hero, role, phase

                         jungle route as camp sequence, recall events

AoV-specific concerns

  • Minimap position. Top-left by default in-game and user-movable. Broadcast observer UI is usually bottom-left and enlarged. Auto-locate per video, never hardcode.
  • Aspect ratios. 16:9, 19.5:9 and 20:9 phones, 4:3 iPad. Anchor the crop to the detected minimap size and train with scale jitter. Switch and PC emulator captures are cleanest.
  • Fog of war. Player-perspective replays hide unseen enemies. Prefer observer or broadcast view, or record both team perspectives as Dota2-Vis did. Verify whether the Garena RoV replay offers a global view.
  • Broadcast overlays. Sponsor bugs, replay wipes, picture-in-picture. Detect a valid minimap per frame via landmark match score, drop invalid frames, let the tracker bridge gaps. Broadcast icons may differ from in-game, so keep a separate template set.
  • Icon clutter. Pings, recall circles, tower flashes, death skulls. Include all as distractors in synthetic data.
  • Patch churn. Hero icons change with patches. Regenerate the synthetic set from new assets.
  • Labeling budget. Synthetic training plus 200 to 500 hand-labeled real crops (CVAT or Roboflow, 2 to 3 hours) for validation and a short fine-tune.

8. Recommended adaptation for the RoV tool

Data

Minimap CV on RPL, AWC and AIC YouTube VODs at 2 fps. Later, screen-recorded scrim replays.

Map model

11 to 14 hand zones: 3 lanes x 3 segments, 4 jungle quadrants, river, Dark Slayer pit, Abyssal Dragon pit, 2 bases. Plus a 12x12 grid for heatmaps.

Core metrics v1

Zone dwell by 3-minute window, zone changes per minute, within-team distance, hull area, jungle and support proximity (threshold as fraction of map width, window 1:30 to 8:00), encounters, recall events, first-2-minute jungle route.

UI v1

Minimap plus timeline scrubber with event jump. Per-player heatmap toggles. Zone dwell table. Two-player path comparison.

Build order

1

OpenCV colour and template baseline on one RPL VOD. Verify crop, coordinate mapping and a jungler heatmap end to end.

2

Synthetic renderer plus YOLO11n, swapped in behind the same interface.

3

Kalman tracking, occlusion rule and validity gating. Then the coaching analytics and the UI.
